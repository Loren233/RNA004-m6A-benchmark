# Ground-Truth Datasets

This directory contains final replicate-intersection ground-truth tables used
by the benchmark. These tables use genomic coordinates and should not undergo
transcript-to-genome conversion.

## HEK293T GLORI Ground Truth

- Cell line: HEK293T
- Method: GLORI
- Ground truth constructed from the intersection of two replicates
- Mean depth and mean stoichiometry are retained

## HeLa eTAM-seq Ground Truth

- Cell line: HeLa
- Method: eTAM-seq
- Comparison: FTOminus versus FTOplus
- Ground truth constructed from the intersection of three replicates
- Mean depth and mean stoichiometry are retained
